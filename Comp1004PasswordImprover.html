How to make a strong password

A strong password isnt about memorising something complicated — its about creating something unpredictable. One of the most reliable approaches is to combine unrelated words, mix in uppercase and lowercase letters, and add numbers or symbols in places that arent obvious. Avoid anything tied to your personal life, like birthdays or pet names, since those are easy to guess. The goal is to build a password thats long, unique, and resistant to common patterns, so even if someone tries to bruteforce or guess it, theyre met with a wall rather than a lucky hit. If you treat your password like a tiny puzzle only you would think to create, youre already ahead of most people.
